3 Days Maasai Mara Safari

A sample safari itinerary — request a personalised quotation for your dates.

Duration3 days / 2 nights
DestinationMaasai Mara, Kenya
Trip typeSafari enquiry

Tour Overview

Experience a safari in the Maasai Mara, known for its open landscapes and abundant wildlife. This sample itinerary gives you a starting point for planning. Accommodation, transport, park fees and final inclusions will be confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1: Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Depart from Nairobi at the agreed time and travel towards the Maasai Mara. Enjoy scenic views along the way, check in to your selected accommodation and proceed with activities according to your final itinerary.

Day 2: Safari experience

Spend the day exploring the reserve on game drives with a qualified guide, subject to park rules and the confirmed schedule. Return to your accommodation after the day's activities.

Day 3: Return to Nairobi

After breakfast and any scheduled morning activity, begin the return journey to Nairobi.
